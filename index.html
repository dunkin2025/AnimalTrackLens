THE PROJECT

Let computers handle the hours of video.

Wildlife cameras, zoo webcams, research cameras, domestic-animal cameras, and commercial monitoring systems can produce enormous amounts of footage. AnimalTrackLens aims to help turn that video into structured behavioral information.

◉

Animal Detection

Locate animals in video and identify the species when the model supports it.

↝

Tracking

Follow animals across frames and measure movement, location, activity, and interactions.

⌁

Behavior Detection

Classify observable activities such as eating, walking, resting, drinking, and social interaction.

▥

Behavioral Analytics

Convert observations into timelines and structured data for research, monitoring, and review.

ANIMALTRACKLENS PIPELINE

Video in. Behavioral data out.

The system is designed as a sequence of measurable stages, from visual detection through tracking and behavior analysis.

01 VIDEO Camera or authorized footage
→
02 DETECTION Find animals in frames
→
03 TRACKING Follow individuals over time
→
04 BEHAVIOR Classify observable actions
→
05 DATA Timeline and analytical output

BEHAVIOR CATEGORIES

Start with behaviors we can observe.

Animal behavior is species-specific. AnimalTrackLens can begin with visually distinguishable behaviors and expand as better datasets and validation become available.

Walking Running Eating Drinking Resting Sleeping Grooming Playing Social interaction Chasing Aggression Nesting Swimming Climbing Other / uncertain

RESEARCH & VALIDATION

AI predictions need human verification.

AnimalTrackLens is intended to assist animal behavior researchers, zoologists, conservation teams, animal welfare specialists, and other professionals. Human annotations and expert review remain essential for defining behaviors, training models, and evaluating results.

Accuracy How often classifications are correct
Precision How reliable positive detections are
Recall How many relevant events are detected
F1 score Balance between precision and recall
